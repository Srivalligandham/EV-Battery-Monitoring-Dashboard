06. Conclusion

The Version 1 battery monitoring system successfully monitors battery voltage, current, temperature, SOC and SOH using ARM Cortex-M4 firmware in QEMU.

Version 2 improves the monitoring approach by introducing Coulomb counting for SOC estimation, capacity-based SOH estimation, median-of-three noise filtering, numerical risk scoring and four-level risk classification. It also uses adaptive monitoring intervals based on the calculated risk.

The simulation results demonstrate the additional monitoring features implemented in Version 2. All results are based on predefined simulated battery samples executed in QEMU.

Overall: Version 2 provides a more detailed and risk-aware battery monitoring approach compared with Version 1.
